## C6 — dropped-call recovery sweep, T4 fan-out entry

`packages/api/test/integration/sweep-tenant-fanout.test.ts` had no entry for
the dropped-call-recovery sweep. `dropped-call-worker.ts` takes no
`listTenantIds` dependency: its repository's `findDueTenantIds`
(`sms/recovery/scheduler.ts`) runs one cross-tenant `SELECT DISTINCT
tenant_id` query — the same shape as the thank-you-SMS and review-request
sweeps already in the file's "cross-tenant query" section, not the
`listTenantIds`-enumerator shape. The new `describe('dropped-call recovery
sweep', ...)` block was added there, using the **real**
`PgDroppedCallRecoveryRepository` and `PgAuditRepository` (no stubs on the
tenant selector), seeding tenants directly through `dropped_call_recoveries`
rows and recording/failing on the handler's `sendSms` seam.
