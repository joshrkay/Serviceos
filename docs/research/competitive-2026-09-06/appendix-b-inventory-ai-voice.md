## Notable gaps I noticed (competitor-advertised AI/voice capabilities I could not find)

**Voice / telephony**
1. **Proactive outbound AI voice agent** — no dialer, no AI-placed calls at all: no speed-to-lead callback, no outbound appointment-confirmation calls, no payment-collection calls, no reactivation/nurture campaigns, no missed-call-back-in-60-seconds voice. Everything outbound is SMS/email/push, plus a human click-to-call bridge. (Tracker row 17 confirms: "only `call_me_back` (reactive)".)
2. **Concurrent-call scaling story** — trial cap is 2 concurrent calls and there's a per-tenant WS lease cap, but no evidence of an "unlimited simultaneous calls" guarantee or queueing/overflow design.
3. **IVR / DTMF menus and keypad input** — `<Gather input="speech">` only; no `input="dtmf"`, no "press 1 for…", no keypad capture for account numbers or card entry.
4. **Voicemail drop / AMD (answering-machine detection)** — no Twilio AMD, no pre-recorded voicemail drop.
5. **Call conferencing / true warm transfer with 3-way** — transfers use `<Dial>` + whisper; there's an explicit note that "there is no separate conference primitive in TwilioCallControl", so no supervised hold-and-brief, no add-a-third-party, no barge/monitor for a human supervisor.
6. **Call hold / music-on-hold / callback-in-queue** — no hold music, no "keep your place in line" callback.
7. **Multilingual beyond en/es** — `Language = 'en' | 'es'` is hard-typed throughout; no French, Mandarin, Vietnamese, Tagalog, and no intra-sentence code-switching (tracker row 14 = 🔴 gap).
8. **Voice cloning / custom brand voice audio** — only three fixed ElevenLabs presets; no tenant voice clone, no per-tenant voice upload.
9. **Objection handling as a graded skill** — tracker row 18 = 🔴; there's a negotiation *refusal* guardrail but no modeled objection-handling or upsell/cross-sell (membership sell, service-plan upsell on the call) capability.
10. **Emergency-vs-routine triage corpus** — tracker row 15 = 🔴 gap; the triage code exists, but there is no golden-script bucket proving it.

**CSR / coaching / QA**
11. **Human CSR call scoring & coaching (Avoca Coach analogue)** — tracker row 19 = 🔴 product gap. The grader infrastructure exists but is pointed only at the AI, not at recorded human agent calls; no scorecards, no coaching feed, no agent leaderboards.
12. **Call-reason/disposition analytics dashboards** — outcomes and lookup events are stored, and `/api/analytics/voice-roi` exists, but there's no missed-call/booking-rate-by-reason breakdown, no per-agent or per-campaign attribution, no call-volume heatmap.
13. **Post-call CSAT / caller survey on the voice channel** — feedback/review requests are SMS/email only; no in-call "rate this call".

**Integrations & data**
14. **FSM write-back (ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro / Jobber / Service Fusion)** — tracker row 20 = 🔴; internal CRM only, no bidirectional field-service-management sync.
15. **Calendar-aware live availability from external calendars during a call** — there's `calendar-integration.ts` / `google-calendar.ts`, but the in-call availability path reads internal appointments; no evidence external busy-time is honored at quote/hold time.
16. **Payment capture on the call** — no PCI-compliant IVR card capture, no pay-by-voice, no deposit taken in-call (deposits ride a send-estimate SMS link).
17. **Number porting / SIP trunking / BYOC** — Twilio-provisioned numbers only.

**Platform**
18. **No-code prompt/persona/script editor for owners** — persona is greeting + agent name + preset voice; the intent taxonomy, prompts, and disambiguation questions are code/pack-owned, and the prompt registry has no admin route.
19. **Live call monitoring / whisper-to-AI / takeover UI** — there's a supervisor "wall" (`/api/voice/sessions/active` + WS) and dispatcher SSE, but no operator-listens-live, no coach-the-AI-mid-call, no seamless human takeover of an in-progress AI call.
20. **A/B testing of greetings/scripts** — shadow comparison covers *models*, not prompts/greetings; no experiment framework for voice copy.
21. **Spam/robocall screening on inbound** — there's spam-create-customer adversarial coverage and blocked-caller-ID handling, but no carrier spam-score check, no CNAM lookup, no known-robocaller blocklist.
22. **Voicemail transcription summarization → typed proposal** — voicemail is transcribed and mints a lead, but I found no path that turns a voicemail transcript into a booking/estimate proposal the way a live call does.
23. **Judge-drift regression pin** (tracker H4) and **transfer-with-context corpus** (row 11) and **quote-accuracy corpus** (row 12) — all named as open in the tracker and still absent from the corpus buckets.
